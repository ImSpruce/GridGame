Tier 1:

resources:
            Gold,
            Wood,
            Stone
Tiles:
            Forest -> Wood
            Stone -> Stone
            Gold -> Gold
            Plains -> buildings

Buildings:
            Lumber Camp -> Wood | needs Forest
            Quarry -> Stone | Needs Stone
            Mine -> Gold | needs Gold Tile
            Monument -> Win Condition


Tier 2:
Population -> Needs Food
Buildings need workers
Houses increase Population Cap
Population grows over time if housing is availabel

-> simple food system no starvation only limits growth

resources deplete if harvested.

Tier 3:

Trade Routes
